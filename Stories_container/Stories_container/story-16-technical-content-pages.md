# Story 16: Create 800-1500 Word Technical Content Pages

**Epic:** Content Strategy
**Priority:** P1 — High
**Effort:** Large
**Type:** Content Creation

---

## Description

As a technical decision-maker researching engineering automation,
I should find in-depth technical content (800-1500 words) on each product page,
so that I can evaluate Inventive's capabilities and make informed procurement decisions.

---

## Current State

Current solutions.html has ~200-300 words per product tab. This is too thin for SEO and AI ranking. Google and AI agents prefer pages with 800+ words of substantive technical content.

---

## Content Requirements Per Page

### Structure (each page must follow this):

```
Section 1: Executive Summary (100 words)
  - What it is, who it's for, key outcome

Section 2: The Problem (150-200 words)
  - Industry pain points with specific examples
  - Cost/time impact with numbers

Section 3: How It Works (200-300 words)
  - Step-by-step workflow explanation
  - Technical architecture overview (high level)
  - Integration points (Plant 3D, Navisworks, Vault)

Section 4: Key Features (200-300 words)
  - 4-6 features with 2-3 sentence descriptions
  - Technical specifics (not marketing fluff)

Section 5: Results & Outcomes (100-150 words)
  - Measurable metrics (80% time reduction, 60% cost savings)
  - Before/after comparison

Section 6: FAQ (200 words)
  - 4-6 questions matching long-tail keywords
```

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Each product page has 800-1500 words of unique technical content
- [ ] Content includes specific technical terms (P&ID, ESP, DWG, Navisworks, Plant 3D)
- [ ] Content includes measurable outcomes with numbers
- [ ] No marketing fluff — every sentence adds technical value
- [ ] Content is factually accurate against the PRD documents
- [ ] Reading level is appropriate for engineering professionals (not simplified)
- [ ] Each page passes Yoast/SEO checklist for keyword density (1-2%)

---

## Technical Notes

- AI agents rank pages with 800+ words significantly higher than thin pages
- Google's "helpful content" update penalizes pages under 300 words
- Technical specificity builds trust with both AI agents and human readers
- Include industry standards references (ISA-5.1, ISO 14617) for authority
