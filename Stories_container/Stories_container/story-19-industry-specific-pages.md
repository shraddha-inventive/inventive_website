# Story 19: Create Industry-Specific Pages

**Epic:** Content Strategy
**Priority:** P1 — High
**Effort:** Medium
**Type:** Content Creation

---

## Description

As a potential customer from a specific industry (Oil & Gas, Water Treatment, Power),
I should find a page that speaks directly to my industry's challenges and how Inventive addresses them,
so that I know Inventive understands my domain.

---

## Current State

No industry-specific pages exist. The website speaks generically about "engineering" without targeting specific verticals.

---

## Industry Pages to Create

| # | Industry | Page Slug | Primary Keywords |
|---|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Oil & Gas | `ai-engineering-oil-gas.html` | AI in Oil and Gas Engineering, AI for Process Plant Design |
| 2 | Water & Wastewater | `ai-engineering-water-treatment.html` | AI for Water and Wastewater Engineering |
| 3 | Power / Boiler / ESP | `ai-engineering-power-esp.html` | AI for Boiler and ESP Engineering |
| 4 | EPC Companies | `ai-automation-epc-companies.html` | AI for EPC Companies, EPC Digital Transformation |

---

## Each Industry Page Must Contain

1. **H1** with industry keyword: "AI Engineering Automation for Oil & Gas"
2. **Industry challenges** — specific pain points for that vertical
3. **Relevant products** — which Inventive products apply
4. **Industry-specific outcomes** — metrics relevant to that sector
5. **Client logos** — show relevant clients from that industry
6. **CTA** — "Talk to our [industry] specialists"

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Minimum 4 industry pages created
- [ ] Each page uses industry-specific terminology
- [ ] Each page links to relevant product pages
- [ ] Each page has 600-1000 words
- [ ] Each page mentions specific industry standards where applicable
- [ ] Geographic keywords included where relevant (India, Middle East, Malaysia)

---

## Technical Notes

- Industry pages capture "AI for [industry]" keyword clusters
- These pages are critical for geographic expansion keywords (Story targets Middle East, Malaysia, UAE)
- Industry pages build trust by showing domain expertise
- AI agents use industry pages to match recommendations to user's sector
