# Story 43: Content Marketing — Guest Blogging, Medium, Dev.to & Industry Blogs

**Epic:** Backlinks & Authority
**Priority:** P2 — Medium
**Effort:** Large (Ongoing)
**Type:** External Activity (No Website Code Changes)

---

## Description

As a company building thought leadership and backlinks,
Inventive should publish technical articles on external platforms,
so that the website earns high-quality backlinks and the company is recognized as an authority in AI engineering.

---

## Platforms & Strategy

| Platform | Content Type | Frequency | Backlink Value |
|----------|-------------|-----------|---------------|
| **Medium** | Technical articles on AI engineering | 2x/month | Medium |
| **Dev.to** | Developer-focused automation tutorials | 1x/month | Medium |
| **LinkedIn Articles** | Industry insights, thought leadership | 2x/month | High (social signal) |
| **Engineering Forums** (Eng-Tips, Reddit r/engineering) | Answers, discussions, helpful posts | Weekly | Low but targeted |
| **Guest Posts** on industry blogs | Detailed engineering automation articles | 1x/quarter | High |

## Each Article Must Include:

- Link back to Inventive website (relevant product page, not just homepage)
- Author bio with company link
- Technical depth (not marketing fluff)
- Relevant keywords naturally embedded
- Call to action: "Learn more at Inventive"

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Medium publication account created
- [ ] Dev.to account created
- [ ] Minimum 3 articles published across platforms
- [ ] Each article includes backlink to relevant Inventive page
- [ ] Articles use consistent author bio and company description
- [ ] Engineering forum accounts created and first posts made

---

## Technical Notes

- Medium articles rank well in Google and are referenced by AI agents
- Dev.to has high domain authority for technical content
- Guest posts on industry blogs (if accepted) provide the highest-quality backlinks
- Consistent publishing builds author authority over time
- No website code changes needed
