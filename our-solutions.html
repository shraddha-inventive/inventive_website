The Evolution of EIT Support Fabrication

From Legacy Manual Drafting to AI‑Driven Generation

The Heavy Ecosystem Trap

Legacy Workflow

Manual 'Snapshot & Redraw'

Engineers manually identify EIT supports and extract geometry one‑by‑one using screenshots, visual inspection, and manual CAD drafting.

Inefficient Manual Drafting

High‑skilled labor drained by low‑value tasks. Project timelines dictated by human headcount rather than computing power.

Software Lock‑in & Data Loss

Dependency on complex software chains (Tekla, SP3D, E3D) causes high‑fidelity 3D data to be lost when flattened manually.

The Rework Loop

Any changes to the upstream model force a manual restart of the entire drafting process from scratch.

Significant Reduction in Manual Effort
Faster Turnaround for EIT Support Drawings

Intelligent Extraction & Generation

AI‑Driven Solution

80% Time on Validation

Engineers elevated to high‑value roles, spending their time on optimization and design intent rather than tracing geometry.

Native Data Ingest from Navisworks

The AI engine bypasses manual exports by directly reading geometry and engineering context from the Navisworks model.

Detect > Analyze > Generate

Automatically scans the model, applies engineering logic (spatial awareness and clearance checks), and generates standardized drawings.

100% Consistent, Fabrication‑Ready

Algorithmic enforcement ensures the first drawing matches the thousandth, delivering clean standardized outputs ready for the shop floor.
