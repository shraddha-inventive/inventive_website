Key Features:

  • Faster and cost-effective digital twin creation
  • Centralized storage of engineering documents
  • P&ID linked 3D visualization and navigation
  • Easy accessibility for users across the organization
  • Complete plant lifecycle management

BIM Services

Comprehensive BIM Services from initial design to project completion.

We offer detailed structural analysis and 3D modeling utilizing Revit, Navisworks, and in-house developed tools to create accurate and efficient BIM models.

Structural BIM

Detailed structural analysis and 3D modeling for accurate and efficient structural BIM models.

Mechanical/HVAC BIM

Detailed 3D models for HVAC systems including ductwork and piping with precise coordination.

Electrical BIM

Accurate electrical system models integrating data and coordinating with other trades.

Plumbing BIM

High-quality 3D models for plumbing systems compliant with industry standards.

Key Capabilities:

  • 2D to 3D BIM Conversion
  • Clash Detection and Coordination
  • LOD 100 to LOD 500 Modeling

  • As-Built Documentation
