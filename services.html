Speed BIM

Tech enabled BIM Model Enhancement

Accelerate BIM model enhancement (LOD 100 to LOD 400) with our advanced in-house tools.

  • Attribute / Parameter Management – Assigning, modifying, and managing parameters across multiple elements
  • Element Renumbering – Renumbering rooms, doors, grids, or other elements
  • Bulk Dimensioning – Adding dimensions to multiple views or elements
  • One-Click Insulation Layer Creation: Automate the addition of insulation layers in your BIM models.
  • Automatic Attribute Migration: Seamlessly transfer attributes from any CAD software to Revit with minimal effort.
  • Faster Model Alignment: Improve accuracy by automatically extracting and aligning model coordinates.

Revit Plug-ins development

Automate your repetitive tasks to fast track your BIM modeling by customized development of Revit Plug ins for your various repetitive tasks.

  • Sheet creation and management – Creating multiple sheets, assigning views, and managing sheet layouts
  • View Template Application – Applying view templates across multiple views
  • Model Cleanup and Optimization – Cleaning up unnecessary elements like removal of unused families, purging unused items, and optimizing the model
  • Clash Detection and Coordination – Identifying and resolving clashes between different disciplines by using Navisworks manage and BIM Track.
  • Family Placement and Arraying – Placing multiple instances of a family (e.g., lights, furniture) by automating the arraying and placement of families based on rules or patterns.
  • Scheduling and Quantity Takeoffs – Creating and updating schedules and quantity takeoffs by using Revit plug ins enabling faster updates and management of schedules and quantities.
  • Batch Exporting and Printing – Exporting or printing multiple views, sheets, or models by automating the batch exporting and printing process.

Digitalization

Digital Handover for Green Field Project using Plant Digital Twin

Problem Statement: Updating as-built engineering documents and ensuring their handover in an easily accessible format is a significant challenge for EPCs (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction companies) and OEMs (Original Equipment Manufacturers). However, it is crucial for both project completion and the entire plant life cycle.

Our Solution: Web enabled P&ID linked Plant Asset's Digital Twin creation

Solution Highlights:

  • Ensure faster and cost-effective creation of Plant digital twin – Centralized storage of engineering documents and data with easy search, access, and collaboration through web browser or QR code scanning.
  • Enables P&ID linked 3D visualization and Navigation of the Plant for users across the organization.
  • Provides updated "as-built" or "as-operated" version of Plant assets with Plant O&M and procurement friendly asset attributes as per the Cobie standards.
