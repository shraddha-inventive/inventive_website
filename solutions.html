AI‑Powered Engineering Platforms

EIT Support Extraction Platform

An AI‑assisted platform that automatically extracts Electrical, Instrumentation, and Telecom supports from 3D models and coordination tools, converting them into fabrication‑ready engineering outputs.

Problem We Solve

EIT supports are typically extracted manually from 3D models or coordination tools using screenshots, visual inspection, and manual drafting in CAD tools.

Key Challenges

  • Highly manual and time‑consuming extraction process
  • Strong dependency on experienced designers
  • High risk of missed or inconsistent supports
  • Rework due to late design changes
  • Difficulty maintaining traceability between model and drawings

Business Impact

  • Delayed fabrication drawings
  • Increased engineering and rework cost
  • Inconsistent support design across projects

Our Solution Approach

The EIT Support Extraction platform works on top of existing 3D coordination environments to:

01

Read and interpret model geometry and metadata

02

Automatically identify EIT‑related supports

03

Classify supports by type, usage, and discipline

04

Generate structured engineering outputs ready for detailing and fabrication

The platform augments existing workflows instead of replacing tools already used by EPCs and contractors.

Key Capabilities

Automated Support Identification

Automatically detects electrical, instrumentation, and telecom supports from 3D models and coordination data.

Intelligent Classification

Classifies supports based on type, discipline, and configuration for consistent downstream use.

Fabrication‑Ready Outputs

Generates drawings and data that can be directly used for detailing and fabrication workflows.

Change‑Friendly Regeneration

Supports quick re‑extraction and regeneration when models change.

Inputs & Outputs

Inputs

  • 3D coordination models (e.g., Navisworks)
  • Electrical, instrumentation, and telecom model data
  • Project standards and support libraries (if available)

Outputs

  • EIT support drawings (DWG / PDF)
  • Support schedules and lists
  • Structured data for further detailing and fabrication
