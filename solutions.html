AI Powered 2D Piping GA from Plant 3D

Automated 2D GA extraction from Plant 3D piping models, enabling automatic generation of production‑ready General Arrangement drawings with integrated sanitization, quality checks, and standardized formatting.

Problem We Solve

Traditional 2D GA creation from Plant 3D involves manual conversion, time‑intensive quality sanitization, and manual formatting to production standards.

Key Challenges

  • Long turnaround time for manual conversion
  • Inconsistent output across drawings and projects
  • High dependency on skilled draftsmen
  • Difficulty generating location drawings
  • Difficulty in coordinating multiple disciplines

Business Impact

  • Delayed project deliverables
  • Increased cost due to rework and manual effort
  • Discrepancies between 3D model and 2D deliverables

Our Solution Approach

Our automation solution works directly within Plant 3D to deliver production‑ready drawings:

01

Works directly in Plant 3D — no model export needed

02

Applies design and drafting standards automatically without manual intervention

03

Auto‑generates production‑ready drawings with tagging, layers, and annotations

04

Built‑in quality checks and easy revision handling
